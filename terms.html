How gifts are used

Gifts support free or discounted haircuts for boys and teens from underserved families in Flint, given at Flint Barber College.

Refunds

If you sent a gift by mistake or sent the wrong amount, call or text 810-241-8162 within 30 days and we will work with you on a refund. Cash App and Zelle payments cannot be reversed by the app once sent, so please confirm the recipient name before you send.

Tax status

Please contact us about the tax treatment of your gift before claiming a deduction.

Website

Content on this site is provided for information about the program. Photos are used with the family's permission.
